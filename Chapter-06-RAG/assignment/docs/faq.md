
# FAQ

## General Questions

### What is your return policy?
We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on all products. Items must be in original condition with proof of purchase.

### How do I track my order?
Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking number via email. Use it to monitor delivery status on our carrier's website.

### Do you offer international shipping?
Yes, we ship to over 150 countries. Shipping costs and delivery times vary by location.

## Account & Payments

### How do I reset my password?
Click "Forgot Password" on the login page and follow the email instructions to create a new password.

### What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit cards, PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay.

### Is my payment information secure?
Yes, we use 256-bit SSL encryption and comply with PCI DSS standards.

## Products & Orders

### How long does delivery take?
Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days. Express shipping is 2-3 business days.

### Can I cancel or modify my order?
You can modify orders within 1 hour of purchase. After that, contact support for assistance.

### Are your products authentic?
All products are sourced directly from manufacturers or authorized distributors.
